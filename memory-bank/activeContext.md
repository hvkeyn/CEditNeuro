v0.67.0. The agent could not read the network from a recording, for three reasons. TCP through the recorder never connected: `new Socket()` has no descriptor before connect, so `protect()` failed and the leg looped into the tunnel; now each leg is a `SocketChannel`, protected before connect, and it reads and writes through the channel (the channel's socket streams share one lock and froze the tunnel loop). Replies were written to the tunnel but not to the pcap; the `Sink` now records both directions. The LAN is outside the tunnel by design, so `net_audit` (study group) reports link, gateway, DNS, private DNS, proxy, open gateway ports, SSDP and mDNS devices, an optional sweep of the phone's own /24 (only ECONNREFUSED counts as alive; Android throws ConnectException for EHOSTUNREACH too), and ranked findings. It only connects and closes; no login, no payload. The pcap summary ends with an audit block: answered and silent TCP calls, cleartext flows, plain DNS, QUIC, and the kind of any secret field in the clear (never the value). `net-map` tells the agent that capture replies are rebuilt (TTL 64 is synthetic) and writes `research/net-audit.md`. MainActivity extra `cedit_audit` (with `sweep`) writes `net-audit.txt` in the app's external files for checks. Checked on the phone: HTTP answered during a recording, 10 of 10 TCP calls answered, `net_audit` found the gateway, 2 SSDP and 4 mDNS devices, 12 hosts in the /24, and SMB and UPnP findings.
